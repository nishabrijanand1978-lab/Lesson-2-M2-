FUN SPACE FACTS

Venus spins backward: Venus rotates in the opposite direction of most other planets, meaning the sun rises in the west and sets in the east.

A day on Mercury is longer than its year: It takes Mercury 88 Earth days to orbit the sun, but 176 Earth days to go from one sunrise to the next.

Diamonds in the sky: Scientists believe that planets like Uranus and Neptune have extreme pressures that rain solid diamonds down into their cores.
